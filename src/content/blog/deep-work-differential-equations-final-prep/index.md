In this session I reviewed key topics to prepare for my Differential Equations final exam. I worked through Laplace transforms and inverse transforms using partial fractions, practiced problems involving step functions and delayed functions, solved IVPs using Laplace methods, and reviewed classifying stability of systems using eigenvalues and the trace-determinant parameter plane. Considerable time spent on partial fractions algebra and understanding when to apply function shifts.

You can find PDF of my notes on my [GitHub](https://github.com/mattcarr17/personal-notes/blob/main/04%20-%20Differential%20Equations%20Finals%20Prep/04%20-%20Differential%20Equations%20Finals%20Prep.pdf?ref=mattcarr.co)
